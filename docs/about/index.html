ABOUT

I’m Abate De Mey, founder of ClinicOS. My background is in robotics and computer vision.

Now

At ClinicOS, we build software to help plastic surgery practices bring in patients, manage consultations, and run their day-to-day operations. We work alongside surgeons to build it.

→ clinicos.ai

Previously

  • FieldVision — I was part of the founding team that built the F1 Camera, an autonomous filming system on the NVIDIA Jetson TX2. It cut the cost of filming youth sports by an order of magnitude.
  • University of Bristol — MSc, Robotics (2016). My dissertation was on absolute position sensing for robotic ball-and-socket joints. It was the top robotics dissertation in my graduating year.
