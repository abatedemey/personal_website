My path

  • ClinicOS (now) — Founder. Building AI infrastructure for plastic surgery practices.
  • BallerTV — Brought automated youth-sports filming to phones, scaling coverage from elite leagues to the broader market.
  • FieldVision — Founding team. Built the F1 Camera, an autonomous filming system on the NVIDIA Jetson TX2 that cut the cost of filming youth sports by an order of magnitude.

Background

The throughline from robotics to ClinicOS is the same: build systems that close the loop between sensing and action. The F1 Camera did it for live sports filming. ClinicOS does it for plastic surgery practices — sensing where a patient is in their journey and acting on it across acquisition, consultation, and clinical operations.

I also helped run the RoboHub podcast, interviewing people building meaningful things in robotics. Selected posts.
