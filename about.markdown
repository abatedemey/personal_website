I'm Abate De Mey, a founder and engineer currently building [ClinicOS](https://clinicos.com).

## Now

Founder of **ClinicOS**, an AI platform for plastic surgery practices that connects patient acquisition, consultation workflows, and clinical operations. Every working hour goes here.

→ [clinicos.com](https://clinicos.com)

## My path

- **ClinicOS** *(now)* — Founder. Building AI infrastructure for plastic surgery practices.
- **BallerTV** — Brought automated youth-sports filming to phones, scaling coverage from elite leagues to the broader market.
- **FieldVision** — Founding team. Built the [F1 Camera](/F1_camera), an autonomous filming system on the NVIDIA Jetson TX2 that cut the cost of filming youth sports by an order of magnitude.
- **University of Bristol** — MSc, Robotics (2016). [Dissertation](/masters-dissertation) on absolute position sensing for robotic ball-and-socket joints — top robotics dissertation in graduating year.

## Background

The throughline from robotics to ClinicOS is the same: build systems that close the loop between sensing and action. The F1 Camera did it for live sports filming. ClinicOS does it for plastic surgery practices — sensing where a patient is in their journey and acting on it across acquisition, consultation, and clinical operations.

I also helped run the **RoboHub podcast**, interviewing people building meaningful things in robotics. [Selected posts](/robohub_posts/).
