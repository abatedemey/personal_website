I'm Abate De Mey, founder of [ClinicOS](https://clinicos.ai). My background is in robotics and computer vision.

## Now

At **ClinicOS**, we build software to help plastic surgery practices bring in patients, manage consultations, and run their day-to-day operations. We work alongside surgeons to build it.

→ [clinicos.ai](https://clinicos.ai)

## Previously

- **BallerTV** — I worked on automated youth-sports filming, getting the algorithms running on phones so more games could be covered.
- **FieldVision** — I was part of the founding team that built the [F1 Camera](/F1_camera), an autonomous filming system on the NVIDIA Jetson TX2. It cut the cost of filming youth sports by an order of magnitude.
- **University of Bristol** — MSc, Robotics (2016). My [dissertation](/masters-dissertation) was on absolute position sensing for robotic ball-and-socket joints. It was the top robotics dissertation in my graduating year.

## Podcast
