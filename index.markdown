## What I'm building

ClinicOS is an AI platform that connects patient acquisition, consultation workflows, and clinical operations for plastic surgery practices. The thesis is that practices need one system that spans the patient journey, not five disconnected tools. We're building it alongside the surgeons who use it.

→ [clinicos.com](https://clinicos.com)

## Why this, why now

Plastic surgery is one of the few healthcare specialties where the practice — not insurance, not a hospital system — owns the patient relationship end-to-end. The consult is the product. The surgeon's time is the constraint. And the journey from first inquiry to procedure is long, high-touch, and almost entirely software-mediated.

Today that software is fragmented, dated, and never designed for how modern practices actually work. AI changes what's possible: patient simulations that drive consult bookings, voice capture that turns conversations into structured plans, photo workflows that don't require a coordinator at every step. The value only compounds if these capabilities live in one system rather than five.

I'm building ClinicOS as a full operating system, not another point tool. In three years, this should be how a modern plastic surgery practice runs.
